# Muse reference variants — prompt set

Mode: built-in ImageGen; eight separate reference-led image edits. Originals retained in Codex generated_images, final PNGs copied here for project use.

Input image: user-supplied Midnight Muse reference. Preserve its fictional adult model, pose, face and center-safe framing; vary wardrobe, light and backdrop. These images contain no baked-in title or subtitle. Editable titles and audio-derived lyrics are separate timeline clips.

## moonlit-muse

Asset: [moonlit-muse.png](./moonlit-muse.png)

Template IDs: creator-moonlit-muse (9:16), creator-moonlit-muse-wide (16:9).

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: photographic background for music lyric-video templates, landscape 16:9.
Input image 1: reference/edit target; preserve the same fictional adult East Asian woman's facial identity, proportions, long wavy hair, gaze, calm expression, and upper-body pose. Do not introduce another person.
Primary request: create a distinct photographic styling variant based on the supplied image.
Composition: preserve the reference framing and subject size, face centered horizontally in the upper third, entire head visible, shoulders ending near the middle. Keep her face entirely in the central third of the width so a center-cropped 9:16 version retains it. Lower half must fade smoothly into clean nearly black negative space for editable lyrics; avoid bright decorations there.
Style: premium photorealistic fashion/editorial photography, realistic skin and hair details. Fully clothed, modest high-neck garment.
Constraints: change wardrobe color/material, backdrop, and lighting only as specified. No baked-in typography, subtitles, graphics, signatures, logos, borders or watermarks. No collage or panels. One finished full-frame image.
Direction: Moonlit silver-blue editorial: midnight navy high-neck silk blouse; cool moonlight key light, subtle silver rim light, deep blue-gray softly blurred studio background. Quiet, wistful cinematic atmosphere.
```
