# Intelligence Creator — abstract lyric artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool (not the API/CLI fallback).
Original generated files remain untouched. These project copies are used as real
image media, saved into IndexedDB and included in project/media exports.
The same artwork is center-cropped with `cover` for portrait or widescreen.

## aurora-silk.png

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: abstract cinematic background for a premium scrolling-lyrics music video template called Aurora Silk.
Primary request: create one beautiful abstract artwork, not a UI mockup. Liquid silk folds and translucent aurora ribbons floating in deep midnight space, lilac and teal light softly glancing across fine satin texture.
Composition: wide 16:9 landscape full-bleed; gentle sculptural forms around the perimeter, calm dark negative space through the center and lower middle so large original lyrics, pinyin and Vietnamese translation remain readable. Also attractive when center-cropped to portrait.
Lighting: refined luminous gradients, cinematic depth, restrained luxury, contemporary dream pop album art.
Constraints: no text, letters, people, logos, watermarks, borders, collage or panels. Avoid busy speckles, hard central highlights, flat SVG-looking shapes. High resolution, clean smooth gradients.
